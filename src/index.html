All our events are self-supported rides on unmarked routes near the Central Coast in California. These rides, or brevets, are on scenic, low-traffic roads whenever possible. The time limits make them challenging too. To learn more about our brevets, go to the menu on the left and click on the various topics. To learn more , go to the website of the Randonneurs USA We are a non-profit organized under section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code.
