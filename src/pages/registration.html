Membership in Randonneurs USA is required before entry in SCR events. Go to: www.rusa.org

All entries must be received by Wednesday evening at 8 PM prior to the event. We're sorry, but we cannot accept any late or day-of entries.

Brevet information, route-sheet, and a link to the RideWithGPS route is sent out via email to all registered riders on the Sunday evening before the event, and again after registration closes.

REGISTRATION IS A 3-STEP PROCESS:

1. Make sure your RUSA membership is current.

2. Sign our waiver and then reply to the email that you receive from SmartWaiver asking for confirmation of your email address.

3. Once you confirm your email address with SmartWaiver, you will be taken to our PayPal payment page to select your event and pay the fee.

You're not done until you've completed steps 1-3.
